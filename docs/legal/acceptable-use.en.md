# Xavier Cloud Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** draft 0.1 · **Date:** [PUBLICATION DATE]

Xavier Cloud exists to back up, sync and query your agents' memory. This policy protects all customers and the shared infrastructure. It is part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.en.md).

## Not allowed

1. **Illegal content:** storing or distributing child sexual abuse material, content infringing copyright or trademarks, unlawfully obtained data or any content prohibited by Colombian law or the law of your country.
2. **Third-party personal data without a legal basis:** uploading other people's personal data without authorization or another valid legal basis, or sensitive data without the safeguards the law requires.
3. **Malicious software:** storing, distributing or running malware, ransomware or tools to compromise other systems.
4. **Attacks and network abuse:** scanning, probing or attacking the Service or other systems; trying to access other customers' accounts or data; bypassing authentication.
5. **Evading limits:** creating multiple accounts, sharing API keys or tampering with the client to get around your plan's storage, device or AI limits.
6. **Unauthorized resale:** reselling or sublicensing the Service, or using it as generic storage for third parties, without a written agreement (Enterprise plan).
7. **Unrelated use:** using the storage as public file hosting, a CDN, for cryptocurrency mining or for workloads other than Xavier memory and backups.
8. **Cloud AI abuse:** using it to generate illegal content or bulk spam, or to try to extract models or other customers' data.
9. **Impersonation:** pretending to be SWAL, another customer or a third party.

## Reasonable use

Each plan's limits are listed on the pricing page. If your usage threatens the Service's stability we may limit it temporarily and will contact you to find the right plan.

## What we do on a breach

Depending on severity: warning, temporary limitation, suspension or account closure. Except for imminent risk or an authority's order, we will notify you first and give you a chance to export your Content. We may report manifestly illegal content to the authorities.

Because Content is stored encrypted and **we do not routinely review it**, we act mainly on reports, technical signs of abuse (for example abnormal usage spikes) or requests from authorities.

## Reporting abuse or vulnerabilities

Write to [ABUSE / SECURITY EMAIL] with as much information as possible. If you find a vulnerability, report it responsibly and do not access other customers' data; we will not take action against good-faith research.
